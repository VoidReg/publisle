# Code of Conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome questions, give constructive feedback, and
focus criticism on ideas and code. Respect people's privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing private
information without consent are not welcome in this project's spaces.

To report a concern privately, email [the maintainer](mailto:coder64bit@gmail.com). Do not post sensitive reports in
public issues. Maintainers will review concerns discreetly, hear relevant
perspectives, and respond proportionately. Responses may include a warning,
removing content, or restricting participation. Maintainers follow these same
expectations.
